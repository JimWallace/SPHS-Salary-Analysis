\section*{Summary}

I was promoted to Professor on July 1, \PoolYear. This report compares my salary with the salaries of all research-stream faculty on the University of Waterloo main campus who were promoted from Associate Professor to Professor in \PoolPrevYear\ or \PoolYear\ ($n = \PoolN$). My annualized base salary of \SubjectBase\ is \SubjectRank\textsuperscript{th} of \PoolN\ (\SubjectPercentile\textsuperscript{nd} percentile). It is \GapMedian\ (\GapMedianPct\%) below the group median of \PoolMedian. Only \NumBelowSubject\ member of the group has a lower salary.\ifUnitComparisons\ Differences in pay between Faculties do not explain my rank: among the \HealthN\ members of the group in the Faculty of Health, my salary is the lowest, and within the School of Public Health Sciences all \SphsNProfAbove\ other Professors were paid more in \PoolYear\ than my annualized base.\fi\ I request a base salary adjustment of \RequestAdj\ (\RequestPct\%). This would bring my salary to \RequestTarget, which is between the lower quartile (\PoolQOne) and the median (\PoolMedian) of the University comparison group.

\section*{Data and Methods}

\begin{enumerate}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item \textbf{Source.} University of Waterloo public salary disclosure lists for \PoolEarlierYear, \PoolPrevYear\ and \PoolYear\ (\href{https://uwaterloo.ca/about/accountability/salary-disclosure}{Salary Disclosure Archives}). Each list gives name, position title and salary paid for the calendar year. I archived each page once and recorded its retrieval date and SHA-256 hash, so the analysis can be repeated exactly.
    \item \textbf{Comparison group.} A person is in the group if their disclosed title changed from ``Associate Professor'' to ``Professor'' between two consecutive lists. This gives \PoolNCurrent\ faculty promoted in \PoolYear\ (\the\numexpr\PoolNCurrent-1\relax\ colleagues and me) and \PoolNEarlier\ promoted in \PoolPrevYear. All \PoolNEarlierKept\ were still listed as Professor in \PoolYear. The group excludes affiliated colleges (Renison, Conrad Grebel, St.\ Jerome's, United College), which are not under the UW--FAUW Memorandum of Agreement; teaching-stream titles; and research, clinical, adjunct and visiting ranks.
    \item \textbf{Matching.} Names were matched across years by exact surname and given name after normalization (upper case, accents, punctuation and middle initials removed). Where two people shared a normalized name, the full name with middle initials was used. Every match that was not exact was checked manually.
    \item \textbf{Measure.} For colleagues, the \PoolYear\ salary paid as disclosed. For me, my annualized base salary. My disclosed \PoolYear\ figure (\$143{,}600) includes about eight months of sabbatical at 85\% pay, so it understates my salary rate.
\ifUnitComparisons
    \item \textbf{Faculty and School comparisons.} Faculty of Health members were identified from the public faculty listings of the Faculty's three units (SPHS, Kinesiology and Health Sciences, and Recreation and Leisure Studies), retrieved \HealthRosterDate. A member of the comparison group was counted only if surname and first given name both matched a listing. SPHS faculty were identified from the public SPHS faculty listing. For both units, this report gives only my rank. It does not report any colleague's salary.
\fi
\end{enumerate}

\section*{Interpretation and Limitations}

\begin{itemize}[leftmargin=*,itemsep=0.2em]
    \item The comparison is descriptive. It does not identify the cause of the difference.
    \item Disclosed salaries are amounts paid in the calendar year. Colleagues' figures can include sabbatical reductions, which lower them, or administrative stipends, which raise them. The median and quartiles are not much affected by a small number of such cases.
    \item My annualized base is a full-year rate. The \PoolYear\ figures for colleagues promoted in \PoolYear\ include about six months at the Associate Professor rate. This makes the comparison conservative: it tends to understate the difference.
    \item The University group does not control for discipline or Faculty, and salary structures differ between Faculties.\ifUnitComparisons\ Within the Faculty of Health, my salary is also the lowest of the recent promotions. That subset is small, and it counts only faculty on current unit listings, so a person who left after promotion is not included.\fi
    \item I hold a cross-appointment in the Cheriton School of Computer Science. Salaries in Computer Science and in the Faculty of Mathematics are therefore a relevant market comparison for my position, and these are among the Faculties included in the University group.
\end{itemize}

\section*{Conclusion and Request}

Among University of Waterloo faculty promoted to Professor in \PoolPrevYear\ and \PoolYear, my salary is near the bottom of the distribution: \SubjectRank\textsuperscript{th} of \PoolN, and \GapMedian\ below the median. \ifUnitComparisons Within the Faculty of Health, my salary is the lowest of the \HealthN\ recent promotions, and within SPHS it is the lowest of the \the\numexpr\SphsNProf+1\relax\ Professors and below \SphsNAssocAbove\ of the \SphsNAssoc\ Associate Professors.\fi

I request a base salary adjustment of \RequestAdj\ (\RequestPct\% of my current base). This would bring my salary to \RequestTarget. That amount is above the lower quartile and below the median of recent promotions across the University. It does not move my salary to the middle of the comparison group.
